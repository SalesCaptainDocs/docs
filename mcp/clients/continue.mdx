Continue connects to the SalesCaptain MCP server natively over streamable HTTP. Once connected, Continue can look up and act on your SalesCaptain calls, contacts, messaging, website leads, payments, reviews, and pipeline stages.

```text
https://external-mcp.salescaptain.com/mcp
```

## Before you begin

You need:

- **Continue** installed in your editor
- A **SalesCaptain** account you can log in to

## Connect Continue

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the server configuration">
    Add the following to your `config.yaml`:

    ```yaml config.yaml
    mcpServers:
      - name: salescaptain
        type: streamable-http
        url: https://external-mcp.salescaptain.com/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>
